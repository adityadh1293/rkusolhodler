Where the yield comes from

$rkuSOL · Earnings

A conventional liquid staking token earns from inflation rewards and, at best, MEV. $rkuSOL stacks a third source on top: the revenue of Raiku's blockspace coordination auctions.

01

Staking rewards

SOL staked with Raiku's validator earns Solana's network inflation rewards. The validator charges 0% commission and 0% MEV commission, so rewards pass through to stakers in full.

02

MEV

Maximal extractable value captured by the validator while producing blocks is shared with the pool, lifting the $rkuSOL exchange rate over time.

03

Auction revenue

Raiku sells guaranteed blockspace through Ahead-of-Time (AOT) and Just-in-Time (JIT) auctions to trading firms that need certain inclusion. That coordination revenue flows back to $rkuSOL holders — a first for a Solana LST.
